projectDescription: Draig Therapeutics is pioneering bold, hopeful research in the treatment of Major Depressive Disorder and other neuropsychiatric conditions, and their website needed to match. With no established web presence to draw from, I began the project by building out a visual language for Draig’s digital space, rooted in the dragon motif from their existing logo. Soft gradients, organic forms, and intentional use of natural imagery evoke the world of neurobiology and bring warmth and optimism to a sensitive subject area. The result is a clean, modern site that honors the emotional weight behind these conditions while reflecting the innovation that defines Draig’s research.
